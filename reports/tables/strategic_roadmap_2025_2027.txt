STRATEGIC ROADMAP 2025-2027
================================================================================

Immediate Actions (Q1-Q2 2025)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Operationalize customer segmentation in CRM and marketing workflows
2. Deploy churn model with optimized threshold (0.4-0.5 range)
3. Implement structured product metadata improvements
4. Pilot cross-channel retention campaigns

Near-Term Priorities (H2 2025)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Implement batch-based ML scoring for weekly targeting
2. Introduce preference center for zero-party data collection
3. Improve return-risk monitoring using transaction patterns
4. Expand omnichannel measurement dashboards

Medium-Term Initiatives (2026)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Move from batch ML scoring to near real-time scoring
2. Integrate segmentation with personalization engine
3. Enhance explainability reporting for executive visibility
4. Build structured sustainability tagging for products

Long-Term Vision (2027)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Develop unified customer intelligence platform
2. Enable AI-assisted personalization workflows
3. Expand omnichannel lifecycle optimization
4. Institutionalize ROI measurement framework across initiatives
